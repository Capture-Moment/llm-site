## What is CaptureMoment?

CaptureMoment is a free and open-source photo editor for Windows, macOS
and Linux. It is made for photographers who want powerful tools without
complexity: a clean interface, professional editing features, and one
guiding idea: edit your photos without ever altering them.

The project is developed in the open under the GNU General Public
License v3. Original photos are never modified: every adjustment is
stored separately and stays reversible at any time.

## Key features

- **Non-destructive editing**: the original file is never touched, every edit stays reversible.
- **Universal RAW files**: open files from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm and many other brands directly, without any conversion.
- **Library**: all photos gathered and organized in one place.
- **Faithful colors**: professional color management from import to export.
- **Fast and fluid**: powered by the computer's hardware, smooth even with large files. The software is developed in C++.
- **Peaceful interface**: nothing superfluous, everything at hand.
- **Cross-platform**: native versions for Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Price and availability

CaptureMoment is completely free: no price, no subscription, no ads,
no account needed. It is open-source software published on GitHub.
